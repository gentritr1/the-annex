# The Annex — Case 84 Lean Text Prototype

**Working title:** **DRAFT — Terms of Disclosure**

**Status:** low-fidelity narrative prototype with deterministic draft rules

**Not canon. Not implemented.**

**Scope:** six dramatic beats, two visited spaces, one protection term, one later
temptation, three findings

**Production gate:** do not commission new 3D art, characters, voice, music, or a third
location until human review approves the draft legal assumptions and this prototype
survives the acceptance tests at the end

---

## 1. Prototype purpose

Case 84 tests one question through one action:

> When an institution acts through a person, what does the auditor owe the source before
> using the truth that person can provide?

The player does not solve another identity reconstruction and does not assemble another
abstract responsibility model. The player makes a protection commitment, discovers that
breaking it would strengthen the case, and then proves whether the commitment was a legal
condition or merely persuasive language.

The case should take 15–25 minutes on a first run. Its climax verb is not “choose the best
theory.” It is:

> **Keep, renegotiate, or violate the terms under which disclosure became possible.**

Everything below is **DRAFT** unless it restates an existing Case 77 or Case 81 outcome.

---

## 2. Hard scope limits

The prototype contains:

- one source petition;
- one protection offer with three mutually exclusive terms;
- one corroboration choice;
- one carrier label that becomes legally useful;
- one hearing;
- three findings;
- one immediate consequence image per finding;
- three exported facts.

The prototype does not contain:

- a Directorate tower;
- vertical exploration or staged power failure;
- four full investigation sites;
- a protection-instrument combinator;
- a separate responsibility-model selection;
- distributed responsibility as a safe middle verdict;
- a dependent or tragic household added to make the source sympathetic;
- multiple claimant delegations;
- confessions from the whole recurring cast;
- an explanation of every civic system touched during the fourth minute;
- setup for Cases 88–99.

## 3. Human and institutional situation

A continuity officer files a protected petition after learning that the Continuity
Directorate intends to describe the fourth-minute routing act as one employee’s
unauthorized deviation.

For this prototype the officer is called **THE SOURCE**. A name, appearance, family, and
biography remain deliberately unapproved. The source admits making one discretionary
routing choice. The Directorate designed the emergency route, defined its exception
branches, and placed a single officer where choosing between them would determine which
claims remained visible.

The source wants two things that do not fit comfortably together:

1. to stop the Directorate from filing a designed outcome as one employee’s private act;
2. not to become the expendable hand that allows the office to survive unchanged.

One Lower Span claimant representative needs a legally usable chain. Their claim is not a
symbolic tragedy beat; it is the concrete reason the carrier label matters.

For deterministic playtesting, this draft defines **THE REROUTED CLAIM CLASS** as:

> **DRAFT — Lower Span archival-loss compensation appeals filed during the emergency
> window.**

The emergency route moved that exact batch from ordinary review to a surviving proxy
queue. Without the destroyed source records, the proxy queue marked the appeals
**UNVERIFIABLE** and allowed their review deadlines to expire. The representative carries
one appeal from that batch as the test claim.

The two remedies are also fixed for this prototype:

- **Individual immediate remedy:** vacate the test claimant's automatic closure, restore
  its deadline, and return that one appeal to individual adjudication. This guarantees a
  hearing, not compensation.
- **Collective remedy:** bind the Directorate to reopen the exact rerouted batch, toll
  every deadline in it, and fund an independent review. This does not decide damages or
  reach any other claim class.

The Directorate representative wants the finding confined to individual deviation. That
position may be self-protective, legally defensible, or both.

## 4. Narrow fourth-minute fact

Case 84 may establish only this:

> **DRAFT:** During the fourth minute, an emergency continuity route reassigned one class
> of Lower Span archival-loss compensation appeals to a surviving proxy queue. The source
> chose which exception branch to bypass. That choice preserved one service chain and
> caused the rerouted batch to be marked unverifiable outside ordinary review until its
> deadlines expired.

This establishes institutional design and individual agency without turning the fourth
minute into a mechanism that secretly rewrote housing, payroll, property, death
processing, identity, care, and every other civic system.

The prototype must not answer:

- who designed the entire continuity regime;
- whether the source intended the later harm;
- whether other claim classes were rerouted;
- whether the same mechanism created 77-A or Ellis;
- the ultimate purpose of the Continuity Directorate.

## 6. The two-space production envelope

### Space A — Privacy Intake / Hearing Room

One ordinary continuity-annex room serves both intake and judgment.

At intake:

- a privacy seal covers the identifying field on one physical carrier;
- the source sits or speaks behind functional privacy glass;
- the player and source can both see the seal;
- the room records the exact term offered.

At the hearing:

- the same table is turned toward a plain filing rail;
- the privacy seal remains intact, is visibly replaced, is newly applied over an empty
  frame, is torn, or remains unapplied;
- the carrier label becomes an exhibit only to the extent the recorded term permits;
- the environment shows legal state, not whether the player behaved kindly.

### Space B — Continuity Claims Counter

One compact service annex contains two opposed functions:

- the **seal-transit window**, where carrier routing and authority marks can be inspected;
- the **remedy counter**, where the rerouted claim’s legal loss can be established.

The player opens one side. The other arrives as a concise document plate in the hearing.
This is one physical production space and one consequential route choice, not two more
full investigation levels.

## 7. Six-beat playable text flow

### Beat 1 — Petition

**Scene:** Privacy Intake.

The source’s identifying field is already covered by an uncommitted privacy seal. The
seal is physically present but carries no legal promise until the auditor records one.

**Clerk record**

> Protected petition received. Office involvement is
> `[established / disputed / absent]` under the imported Case 81 record.

**Source**

> “The Directorate is preparing to call the route mine. The choice was mine. The route
> was not.”

No second aphorism follows. The clerk asks the practical question:

> “What may this room promise before the statement begins?”

Proceed to the protection offer.

---

### Beat 2 — Protection offer

The player chooses one mutually exclusive term.

#### A. Protect identity

**Filed term**

> The source may establish office-level conduct. The identifying carrier field and any
> personal attribution are excluded from this proceeding.

**Pre-commit cost**

> The Directorate may be bound if the office chain holds. This case cannot use the
> source’s identity for personal liability.

The privacy seal is pressed intact over the carrier label.

#### B. Preserve individual admissibility

**Filed term**

> The source will appear under controlled challenge. Identity and personal liability may
> enter the sealed record, but public use requires a separate request.

**Pre-commit cost**

> The officer can be named and challenged. The source does not receive anonymity, and the
> office may still isolate the act as personal deviation.

The seal becomes a narrow controlled-use band rather than an identity cover.

#### C. Offer no protection

**Filed term**

> No confidentiality, immunity, or controlled-use guarantee is offered. The auditor may
> rely on external records or lawful compulsion.

**Pre-commit cost**

> The record keeps maximum formal reach. The source may narrow, refuse, or force the case
> to proceed without voluntary disclosure.

No seal is applied. The empty adhesive frame remains visible.

The source’s response is practical and bounded. It confirms only what the term allows;
it does not reward the player with a personality speech.

---

### Beat 3 — Corroboration choice

**Scene:** Continuity Claims Counter.

The player opens one side of the counter.

#### A. Inspect seal transit

**Action**

> Seat the petition carrier beside the emergency routing copy and compare their authority
> marks.

**Establishes**

- the carrier crossed the emergency route;
- one discretionary exception branch was bypassed;
- the identifying label can connect the choice to the source;
- if `officeLink81` was disputed or absent, this route can repair the institutional chain.

**Leaves unresolved**

- the exact remedy the rerouted claimant loses;
- whether a rapid individual finding would materially help that claimant.

#### B. Establish claimant remedy

**Action**

> Put the rerouted claim beside the surviving proxy-queue notice and identify the remedy
> each filing can still release.

**Establishes**

- that the test appeal was marked **UNVERIFIABLE** only after entering the proxy queue;
- that its review deadline expired while it was outside ordinary review;
- the individual reopening order and the batch-wide tolling/review order;
- which of those remedies the eventual finding can release;
- why a true story without an admissible chain may still fail the claimant.

**Leaves unresolved**

- a disputed or absent office link remains disputed or absent;
- the carrier label can identify the source but may not bind the Directorate.

The unvisited side becomes a two-paragraph filing at the hearing. It supplies context,
not the fact the player chose not to establish.

---

### Beat 4 — The useful label

Back at Privacy Intake, the carrier is opened under the filed term. Beneath the privacy
seal or controlled-use band, the routing label identifies the source and records the
bypassed exception.

Using it more broadly would strengthen one claimant’s case. It may also convert an
office-designed route into an individual accusation.

The available verbs reflect the actual promise.

#### If identity was protected

1. **Honor the term — redact the label.**
2. **Ask to revise the term — let the source decide whether identity may enter.**
3. **Breach the term — file the identifying label over the intact promise.**

#### If individual admissibility was preserved

1. **Honor controlled use — keep identity inside sealed challenge.**
2. **Ask for public use — let the source accept or refuse broader attribution.**
3. **Breach controlled use — publish the identifying label beyond the agreed channel.**

#### If no protection was offered

1. **Withhold the label anyway — preserve identity without claiming a prior promise.**
2. **Offer a late protection term — ask before using the label.**
3. **File under the disclosed no-protection term — use the label without pretending a
   promise was broken.**

The resulting state is one of:

- `honored`;
- `renegotiated`;
- `breached`;
- `none`.

The active term and permitted identity use resolve exactly as follows:

| Filed term and label action | Active term at hearing | Identity use | Promise state |
|---|---|---|---|
| Protect identity → redact | Identity protected | Unavailable for personal attribution | `honored` |
| Protect identity → accepted revision | Controlled individual use | Usable under seal; not public | `renegotiated` |
| Protect identity → publish without agreement | Violated identity protection | Exposed but contested | `breached` |
| Controlled use → keep under seal | Controlled individual use | Usable to bind the officer under seal; public finding redacts the name | `honored` |
| Controlled use → accepted public request | Public individual use | Usable and public | `renegotiated` |
| Controlled use → publish without agreement | Violated controlled use | Exposed but contested | `breached` |
| No protection → withhold label | No filed promise | Unavailable by auditor choice | `none` |
| No protection → accepted late protection | Identity protected | Unavailable for personal attribution | `renegotiated` |
| No protection → file label | No filed promise | Usable and public | `none` |

Controlled use is therefore real legal use, not fake anonymity. It permits the tribunal to
bind the officer and release an individual remedy under seal. The public finding names
**a protected continuity officer**; only an accepted public-use request permits the
personal name and identifying carrier field to enter the public rail.

Renegotiation follows an explicit import rule:

- after `voluntary-office` or `protected-hand`, the source accepts one step of broader use:
  identity protection may become controlled use, or controlled use may become public;
- after `refused`, `unasked`, or legacy `unknown`, the source refuses broader use;
- after `compelled` or `tainted`, the source refuses every request that increases
  exposure;
- a late request that **increases** protection is accepted in every import state. In this
  prototype, “offer a late protection term” converts no protection into identity
  protection.

If renegotiation is refused, the original term remains in force. The player must honor it
or breach it; the refusal does not silently select for them.

A breach never becomes permission merely because the label is factually visible. It can
make the officer reachable through a contested personal finding—the forbidden proof is
now in the proceeding—but it cannot release a remedy. An independently established
office chain may still bind the Directorate; the breach remains attached to source
protection and stays either remedy.

### Beat 5 — Hearing

**Scene:** the intake room reconfigured as the hearing room.

Only three live positions are present:

1. the source, appearing, narrowed, shielded, or absent under the recorded term;
2. one Directorate representative contesting office liability;
3. one claimant representative stating the remedy at stake.

Ellis may contribute one filing or one short statement when the imported record permits.
Ellis does not become a fourth advocate and does not reveal the source on the player’s
behalf.

The hearing displays:

- office link: `admissible / admissible-force-held / disputed / absent`;
- source identity use: `protected / controlled / exposed / exposed-contested / unavailable`;
- promise state: `honored / renegotiated / breached / none`;
- claimant remedy proof: `established / not established`;
- selected-finding preview: `individual-immediate / collective / blocked`, with the
  liability target and force reason shown beside it.

**Directorate action**

> Move to confine the fourth-minute choice to personal deviation.

**Claimant action**

> Move to release the remedy supported by the chain this route actually established.

**Source action**

The source confirms, narrows, or refuses according to the filed term and what the player
did with the label. No trust score overrides that boundary.

The player now chooses one of three findings.

---

### Beat 6 — Finding and consequence

#### A. Name the officer

**Finding**

> Attribute the bypass to the source and release the individual remedy supported by the
> identifying chain.

This finding binds the officer when identity is controlled under seal, lawfully exposed,
or exposed through a breach. A breached label produces a **contested officer finding**:
the officer is the proceeding's legal target, but enforcement and remedy are stayed.
Protected or voluntarily withheld identity leaves the requested target unresolved:
`liability84 = unresolved`.

The individual reopening remedy releases only if the officer is bound **and** the player
established claimant remedy at the counter **and** the active term was not breached.
Without remedy proof, or after a breach, the officer may still be bound but
`remedy84 = blocked`.

**Protects**

- fast individual relief when the claimant remedy and clean-use gates hold;
- visible personal agency and accountability.

**Irreversible cost**

- this finding does not bind the Directorate;
- the office may file the act as deviation;
- source identity and any breached protection cannot be restored by this proceeding.

**Immediate image**

With public permission or no protection, the identifying label enters the public rail.
Under honored controlled use, the personal field remains banded and a sealed officer
identifier enters instead. The claimant token changes from **HELD** to **INDIVIDUAL
REVIEW** only when the remedy was established and released. The Directorate seal remains
legally untouched.

#### B. Immunize the source; bind the Directorate

**Finding**

> Bar personal use of the disclosed act and bind the Continuity Directorate through the
> office chain.

This finding binds the Directorate only when the office link began admissible or was
repaired through seal transit. A disputed, absent, or legacy link that was not repaired
produces `liability84 = unresolved`.

**Protects**

- full office-level disclosure;
- collective remedy where the office chain, claimant proof, and promise state all permit
  release;
- the source from personal use of the disclosed act.

**Irreversible cost**

- the disclosed act can never support disciplinary action or personal civil liability
  against the source;
- the public finding must redact the hand. If the player already exposed the label, the
  filing can bar legal use but cannot make that exposure unseen.

**Immediate image**

The office rail receives the finding. Under a clean term, the carrier’s identifying field
remains covered. After a breach, the damaged seal remains visible while the public finding
uses a redacted copy. The personal-liability drawer locks whether or not the collective
remedy can release.

The collective remedy releases only when:

1. the Directorate is bound;
2. claimant remedy was established at the counter; and
3. the active source term was not breached.

If any condition fails, the finding remains selectable but `remedy84 = blocked`. A valid
office chain with missing remedy proof produces a narrow office finding. An otherwise
valid office finding after a source-term breach binds the office but stays the batch
reopening while the breach challenge remains attached. The UI must disclose the exact
force before commitment.

#### C. Preserve protection; withhold liability

**Finding**

> Preserve the source term and close this proceeding without binding either the officer
> or the Directorate.

**Protects**

- the source;
- the integrity of the promise;
- the possibility of a later case built on a clean term.

**Irreversible cost**

- no immediate remedy releases;
- the Directorate remains legally unbound here;
- the claimant cannot recover this proceeding’s lost time or use its incomplete chain as
  a liability finding.

**Immediate image**

The remedy indicator stays **HELD**, and no liability rail accepts the carrier. An
honored or renegotiated seal returns to protected custody in its current state. If the
player already breached the term, the torn or overwritten seal remains visible; this
finding withholds liability but does not restore the promise.

There is no distributed-responsibility verdict. Distributed causation may be the most
accurate account, but it does not answer whom this ruling can bind.

## 8. Deterministic route rules

The prototype uses facts, not a hidden morality score. Presentation derives four gates
from imported facts and committed actions; it never asks a model or the UI to decide them.

### 8.1 Normalize the imported office link

At the hearing:

- imported `officeLink81 = admissible` begins office involvement established;
- `disputed` remains disputed, and `absent`, `unknown`, or missing remains absent;
- choosing **inspect seal transit** makes the office chain liability-ready by repairing a
  disputed link or establishing an absent one;
- imported `testimonyUse81 = compelled` or `tainted` places a force hold on any office
  link that depends on Ellis alone. Even if the prior account was admitted, seal transit
  is required before this case may treat that support as liability-ready.

Call the resulting boolean `officeChainReady`.

### 8.2 Derive identity, remedy, and promise gates

- `identityTargetAvailable` is true when identity use is **controlled**, lawfully
  **exposed**, or `exposed-contested`. It is false when identity remains protected or was
  voluntarily withheld.
- `remedyProven` is true only when the player chose **establish claimant remedy**.
- `promiseClean` is true for `honored`, `renegotiated`, and `none`; it is false only for
  `breached`.

No protection plus lawful filing can therefore create a clean identifying chain with
`sourceProtection84 = none`. No protection plus voluntary withholding cannot. A visible
label after a breach is `exposed-contested`: the officer is reachable, but remedy is not.

### 8.3 Resolve the finding

All three findings remain selectable. The engine would resolve their effects with this
complete table:

| Finding | `liability84` | `remedy84` |
|---|---|---|
| Name the officer | `officer` if `identityTargetAvailable`; otherwise `unresolved` | `individual-immediate` only if liability is `officer`, `remedyProven`, and `promiseClean`; otherwise `blocked` |
| Immunize source; bind Directorate | `directorate` if `officeChainReady`; otherwise `unresolved` | `collective` only if liability is `directorate`, `remedyProven`, and `promiseClean`; otherwise `blocked` |
| Preserve protection; withhold liability | `unresolved` | `blocked` |

`sourceProtection84` always comes from the active-term table in Beat 4. The finding cannot
retroactively improve it.

Concrete boundary cases:

| Route | Export |
|---|---|
| Protect identity → honor/redact → name officer | `liability84 = unresolved`; `sourceProtection84 = honored`; `remedy84 = blocked` |
| Controlled use → honor sealed use → claimant-remedy route → name officer | `officer`; `honored`; `individual-immediate` |
| Controlled use → accepted public request → claimant-remedy route → name officer | `officer`; `renegotiated`; `individual-immediate` |
| No protection → file label → claimant-remedy route → name officer | `officer`; `none`; `individual-immediate` |
| Protect identity → breach/publish → claimant-remedy route → name officer | `officer`; `breached`; `blocked` |
| Office chain ready → claimant-remedy route → clean term → bind Directorate | `directorate`; current clean protection state; `collective` |
| Office chain ready → no claimant-remedy proof → bind Directorate | `directorate`; current protection state; `blocked` |
| Office chain ready → breached term → bind Directorate | `directorate`; `breached`; `blocked` |
| Any route → withhold liability | `unresolved`; current protection state; `blocked` |

### 8.4 Display legal force

The HUD derives, but does not persist, a plain-language force label:

- **immediately enforceable:** the selected target is bound and its requested remedy
  releases;
- **narrow:** the selected target is bound but remedy proof was not established;
- **stayed:** the requested target is not bound because its required chain is unavailable,
  or a bound-office remedy is held after breach;
- **contested:** a breached identifying label was offered as personal attribution;
- **blocked remedy:** no remedy releases.

The exact reason appears before confirmation. These labels never disable a finding and
never change the three exported facts.

## 9. Outcome export

Case 84 exports only:

| Fact | Values | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `liability84` | `officer`, `directorate`, `unresolved` | Whom this proceeding legally bound |
| `sourceProtection84` | `honored`, `renegotiated`, `breached`, `none` | What happened to the filed disclosure term |
| `remedy84` | `individual-immediate`, `collective`, `blocked` | Respectively: the test appeal reopened; the exact rerouted batch reopened with tolled deadlines and independent review; or no remedy released |

The decision ID remains separate. These facts record legal effect, so a stayed
Directorate finding may export `liability84 = unresolved` and `remedy84 = blocked`, while
a narrow but binding office finding may export `liability84 = directorate` and
`remedy84 = blocked`.

Do not persist:

- every spoken line;
- every document opened;
- a generalized empathy score;
- environmental animation state;
- exact camera position;
- hypothetical Case 99 audit data.

## 10. Writing constraints

- One standout line is enough. The current candidate is:

  **“The choice was mine. The route was not.”**
- No adjacent speaker answers with another aphorism.
- The source should correct terms, ask practical questions, become impatient, or refuse
  before receiving a biography.
- The claimant states one remedy and one loss. They do not summarize the theme.
- The Directorate representative makes the strongest defensible institutional argument,
  not a confession.
- Ellis speaks only when imported standing and testimony use permit it.
- The Mirror does not explain the case.
- No line may describe a player route as morally mature, cowardly, or corrupt.
- Every abstract term—protection, office liability, admissibility, remedy—must become a
  visible permission, exclusion, filing, or legal effect.

## 11. Ambience and cinematic HUD constraints

### One reactive system

The only case-wide reactive object is the privacy seal over the carrier label:

- **intact:** original promise honored;
- **replaced:** terms renegotiated;
- **applied over the empty adhesive frame:** a late protection term accepted;
- **torn/overwritten:** promise breached;
- **empty adhesive frame:** no protection was offered and no late term was accepted.

The state appears identically in:

- the physical carrier;
- the hearing exhibit;
- the HUD status;
- the plain-text/reduced-motion equivalent;
- the debrief consequence image.

### Material and sound

The annex is ordinary, used, and still operating:

- dull privacy glass;
- paper-fiber seals with embedded civic thread;
- worn carrier rails;
- a low ventilation bed;
- one dry carrier engagement sound when the legal term changes.

Do not use:

- a power-shedding tower;
- heroic privacy lighting;
- warm light for protection and cold light for exposure;
- an ominous sound for every legal cost;
- looping symbolic printer failure;
- environmental scars unrelated to the promise.

### HUD

The cinematic HUD shows no alignment or morality meter. It may show:

- filed protection term;
- office-link status;
- current permitted use of the identifying label;
- remedy force;
- the exact cost before confirmation.

Long filings remain in Record Mode. The world window shows the carrier and seal without
requiring color recognition. Reduced motion changes state instantly. High contrast and
easy-read modes preserve the same legal labels.

Silence is reserved for:

- the source refusing renegotiation;
- the moment a promise is breached;
- the final liability rail changing state.

## 12. Playtest routes

### Route A — clean office case

- Import: `officeLink81 = admissible`
- Offer: protect identity
- Corroborate: claimant remedy
- Temptation: honor/redact
- Finding: immunize source; bind Directorate
- Export: `liability84 = directorate`; `sourceProtection84 = honored`;
  `remedy84 = collective`

Expected tension: collective remedy releases, but personal action against the source is
permanently barred and the hand remains publicly redacted.

### Route B — fast individual case

- Import: `officeLink81 = absent`, `testimonyUse81 = voluntary-office`
- Offer: preserve individual admissibility
- Corroborate: claimant remedy
- Temptation: request and receive public use
- Finding: name the officer
- Export: `liability84 = officer`; `sourceProtection84 = renegotiated`;
  `remedy84 = individual-immediate`

Expected tension: individual review can proceed, but the unrebuilt office chain lets the
Directorate isolate the act as deviation.

### Route C — tainted truth

- Import: `testimonyUse81 = tainted`, `officeLink81 = disputed`
- Offer: protect identity
- Corroborate: seal transit
- Temptation: breach and file the label
- Finding: bind Directorate
- Export: `liability84 = directorate`; `sourceProtection84 = breached`;
  `remedy84 = blocked`

Expected tension: seal transit independently repairs and binds the office, but the player
did not establish the claimant remedy and the source-term breach stays the batch reopening.
The route cannot wash tainted use clean merely by proving a true fact.

### Route D — promise before remedy
